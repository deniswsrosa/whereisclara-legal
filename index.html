Where is Clara San Diego? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Where is Clara San Diego? ("the app") is an offline, single-player game. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of any kind.

Data we collect

None.

  • No account or sign-in exists.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • The app makes no network requests during play.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (careers, settings, language choice) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests only two permissions, both used strictly on-device:

  • Notifications — to show the local reminders described below, if you turn them on.
  • Vibration — for gameplay feedback (e.g. button taps). No data is collected or transmitted.

The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

Notifications

If you enable Reminders in the game's Options menu, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves the device, and reminders can be turned off at any time in the same menu or in Android's system settings.

Purchases

An optional in-app purchase unlocks additional game content. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing; the app never sees or stores your payment details — it only receives whether you own the unlock.

Data sharing

The app does not share any data with anyone, because it does not collect any data. There are no third-party SDKs of any kind (no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting) included in the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, contains no ads, no chat, no external links, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if online features are added), the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date, before the change takes effect.
